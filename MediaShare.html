            How to request
            
      Requests are made on Twitch, not here.

            
      Free: type !ms <link> in chat, or just paste a link.
            
      Bits: cheer 100+ bits with the link in the message (250 bits = 1 sub).
            
      Sub: put the link in your resub message. First-time subs can't add a message: subscribe, then post !ms <link> and your priority slot is applied.
            
      Channel points: redeem the reward with the link as your message.
            
      Tips/donations: put the link in the donation message and donate under your Twitch name.
            
      Higher paid amounts go first, then channel points, then free. Nothing interrupts the song that is playing.
            
      Supported: YouTube links and direct audio/video files.
            Each person can have a limited number of requests waiting, and a maximum video length, depending on their role. Type !ms limits in chat to see them.
